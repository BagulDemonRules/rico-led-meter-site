Privacy Policy

Rico LED Meter

Updated October 24, 2026

Overview

Rico Softwares designed Rico LED Meter to display audio-input levels without collecting personal information. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking technology.

Audio input

With your permission, Rico LED Meter accesses the audio input selected in macOS solely to calculate and display real-time levels. Audio is processed locally on your Mac. The app does not record, store, transmit, or share audio.

Data collection and sharing

Rico LED Meter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not send app activity over the internet.

Local preferences

The selected visual theme may be stored locally on your Mac so the app can remember your choice. This preference remains on the device and is not transmitted to Rico Softwares.

If you enable “Open Rico LED Meter at login”, macOS manages that registration on your Mac. You can disable it in the app’s Settings or in macOS Login Items settings.

This website and support email

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party trackers added by Rico Softwares. Hosting is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement; the app’s local audio processing is separate from visiting this website.

If you contact support by email, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive audio recordings.

Children’s privacy

Because Rico LED Meter does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Policy updates

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
