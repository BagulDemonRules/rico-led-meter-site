Rico LED Meter

Rico LED Meter displays the live level of the audio input selected in macOS. It does not capture the sound playing from other apps or the system output.

Setup and troubleshooting

Select an audio input

Open System Settings → Sound → Input, then select the microphone, audio interface, or other input device you want to monitor.

Allow microphone access

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and enable Rico LED Meter. Quit and reopen the app after changing this permission.

If the meters do not move

  • Confirm that the selected input device is receiving a signal.
  • Check the device input level in System Settings.
  • Reconnect an external audio interface, then reopen the app.

Channels and themes

Stereo inputs display separate left and right levels. A mono input may display the same level on both sides. Choose among Rainbow, Purple Rain, Blue Ice, Broadcast, Vintage, and Red Alert.

Open at login (version 1.1.0)

Open Rico LED Meter → Settings, or press Command-comma. Enable “Open Rico LED Meter at login” to launch the app when you sign in to your Mac. Disable the checkbox to stop automatic launch. If approval is required, follow the instructions shown in Settings.

Closing the app (version 1.1.0)

Closing the main meter window quits Rico LED Meter completely, just like Command-Q. Reopen the app from Applications or the Dock. Your selected theme is remembered.

Requirements

Rico LED Meter requires macOS 26.0 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel-based Macs.

Contact support

When contacting support, include your macOS version and the name of the audio input device you are using.
